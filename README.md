### CLIM-000229: Aerosol-induced cloud adjustments beyond ship tracks
- **Subfield:** Radiative forcing, clouds, and climate sensitivity
- **Open:** Quantify aerosol effects on cloud fraction, liquid water, precipitation, and radiation after clouds fully adjust. Develop observational constraints transferable beyond short-lived, visibly selected ship tracks.
- **Status:** Open for decades; Glassmeier et al. (2021) showed that ship-track extrapolation can overestimate climatological cooling because slower adjustments matter.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Constrains warming unmasked as air pollution declines.
- **Key works:** Glassmeier et al. (2021), [“Aerosol-cloud-climate cooling overestimated by ship-track data”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abd3980).
- **Related:** Selection bias in natural experiments; droplet population-balance modeling.
